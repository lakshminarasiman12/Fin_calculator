    GLOSSARY
    TDS
    Tax deducted at source; the estimate here is illustrative and depends on the applicable rules and depositor circumstances.
    PAN
    Permanent Account Number used for tax identification in India.
    EAY
    Effective annual yield after accounting for compounding within a year.
    Compounding
    Interest is added to principal and can earn interest in later periods.
    Form 15G / 15H
    Declarations that eligible taxpayers may submit to request non-deduction of TDS; eligibility rules apply.
    Basis point
    One hundredth of a percentage point (0.01%).
